# Invoicing

- Invoices are issued on the 1st of each billing month for monthly plans, or
  annually in advance for annual plans.
- Invoices are sent by email in PDF format and are also available in the
  customer's billing portal.
- Payment terms are 14 days net from invoice date for existing customers,
  and due on receipt for the first invoice of a new contract.
- Late payments beyond 30 days may result in automation pausing until the
  balance is settled; customers are notified at 7, 14, and 30 days overdue.
- All invoices are in EUR. VAT is applied according to the customer's
  registered country within the EU; reverse charge applies for valid VAT
  numbers outside the billing country.
- Enterprise customers (Scale tier) can request consolidated quarterly
  invoicing instead of monthly.
