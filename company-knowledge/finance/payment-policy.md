# Payment & Refund Policy

## Accepted payment methods
SEPA direct debit and credit card for monthly plans; bank transfer or credit
card for annual plans.

## Refund policy
- **Implementation fee**: non-refundable once discovery work has started.
- **Subscription fees**: pro-rated refund available within the first 14 days
  of a new contract ("cooling-off period") if the customer has not yet
  activated a live automated workflow.
- **Enterprise (Scale tier) customers**: refund requests are handled
  case-by-case by the account manager; a human must always review these,
  regardless of AI confidence — do not auto-approve enterprise refunds.
- After 14 days, no refunds are issued for the current billing period, but
  customers can cancel future renewals at any time from the billing portal.

## Disputes
Any payment dispute or chargeback must be escalated to a human immediately;
this is never handled automatically.
