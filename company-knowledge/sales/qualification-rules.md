# Lead Qualification Rules

## Scoring factors (0-100)
- **Budget stated and within a viable tier** (see pricing.md): up to +35 points
- **Company size 15-200 employees**: up to +25 points
- **Explicit buying intent** ("we want to automate", "looking for a system",
  "can we schedule a call"): up to +25 points
- **Urgency signals** ("next week", "as soon as possible"): up to +15 points

Requests with no stated budget and no clear buying intent (e.g. general
questions, support requests) should score below 30.

## Priority mapping
- Score 80-100: **high** — schedule a discovery call within 48h, notify
  #sales-leads on Slack immediately
- Score 40-79: **medium** — send a personalized info email, follow up in
  5 business days if no reply
- Score 0-39: **low** — send a standard info email, no proactive follow-up

## Company size fit
- Under 15 employees: recommend Starter tier, flag as lower priority unless
  urgency is high
- 15-60 employees: Growth tier is the default recommendation
- 60+ employees: Scale tier, involve a human sales rep before quoting price
