# Pricing

All prices are in EUR, excluding VAT, for annual contracts. Monthly billing is
available at a 15% premium.

## Starter — €490/month
- Up to 15 employees using the platform
- Single automated workflow (e.g. support triage or lead qualification)
- Email support, 48h response time
- Shared knowledge base (up to 50 documents)

## Growth — €1,200/month
- Up to 60 employees
- Up to 5 automated workflows across sales, finance, and operations
- Slack + CRM integration included
- Dedicated knowledge base with RAG search
- Priority support, 24h response time

## Scale — €2,800/month
- Unlimited employees
- Unlimited workflows, custom department routing
- Dedicated success manager
- Custom integrations (ERP, internal tools)
- SLA: 99.9% uptime, 4h response time for critical issues

## One-off implementation fee
A one-time setup fee applies based on complexity:
- Starter: €800
- Growth: €2,500
- Scale: €6,000+ (scoped individually)

## Typical budget fit
Companies with 20-60 employees and an automation budget between €8,000 and
€20,000/year for the first year (implementation + 12 months) are the best fit
for the Growth tier. Budgets below €5,000/year should be pointed to the
Starter tier.
