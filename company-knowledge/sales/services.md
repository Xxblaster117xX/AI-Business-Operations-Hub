# Services

We build and operate AI-driven business automation for small and mid-size
companies. Core service lines:

## Support automation
Automates first-line customer support: classifies incoming tickets, answers
from the company knowledge base, and escalates to a human when confidence is
low. Typical deployment: 2-3 weeks.

## Lead qualification & sales automation
Ingests inbound leads (web forms, email), scores them, drafts a personalized
first response grounded in your pricing and services docs, and routes high
value leads to the sales team via CRM + Slack alerts. Typical deployment:
2-4 weeks.

## Finance operations automation
Automates routine finance questions (invoicing status, payment policy,
refund eligibility) and drafts responses for customer-facing finance
questions, always requiring human approval for anything involving an actual
payment or refund amount.

## Operations & escalation automation
Classifies internal operational requests, applies the escalation policy, and
notifies the right on-call channel.

## What's included in every engagement
- Discovery workshop to map your existing processes
- Custom knowledge base ingestion (your docs, not generic templates)
- AI guardrails: every automated action has a confidence threshold; anything
  below it is routed to a human for approval before it goes out
- Analytics dashboard showing automation rate, time saved, and cost per
  request
